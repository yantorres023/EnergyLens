## Summary

EnergyLens works entirely on your phone. We (the developer) do not receive, collect, store or sell any of your information. There is no account, no server, no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.

## What the app stores on your device

* Figures you enter or confirm from your electricity bills: billing dates, kWh, unit rates, standing charges, VAT rate, credits and charges, bill total, account balance, meter-reading type, and optionally a supplier name.
* Household choices: meter type, tariff type, payment method, optional Direct Debit amount, optional region.
* Actions you choose to track and their results.
* If you switch it on (off by default): a private usage log of which features you used. It contains no amounts, dates or free text and never leaves your device unless you choose to share it.

The app does **not** ask for or store your name, address, account number or meter point number (MPAN). If you paste text from a bill, it is read on your device to find bill figures and then discarded; it is not saved.

## What leaves your device

Nothing, automatically. The Android app requests no internet permission. If you take a screenshot or share information yourself, that is under your control.

## Backups

On Android, EnergyLens excludes its data from cloud backups and device transfers. On iPhone, app data may be included in your own device backups (iCloud or computer), which are controlled and encrypted by Apple and you.

## Your control

* Edit or delete any bill in the app.
* Settings → "Delete all my data" erases everything the app stores.
* Uninstalling the app deletes its data.

Because we hold no data about you, we cannot access, export or delete it on your behalf; you have full control on your device. You can still contact us at [email] with questions. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

## Children

EnergyLens is intended for adults responsible for household bills.

## Changes

If a future version adds any feature that sends data off your device, we will update this policy and ask for your consent in the app before it is used.
