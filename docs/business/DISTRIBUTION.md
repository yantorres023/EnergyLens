# Distribution

All channel assumptions are HYPOTHESES. No channel has been tested.

## Timing is the strategy

Demand spikes predictably: cap announcements (late Feb, May, Aug, Nov), cap effective dates (1 Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct), first bills after those dates (~2–6 weeks later), the VAT cut's first bills (Oct–Nov 2026), and its scheduled end (1 Apr 2027). Plan content and partner outreach around these windows.

## Channels

| Channel | Hypothesis | First test |
|---|---|---|
| SEO | People search the exact question after a surprising bill | 10 evergreen pages on the landing site; measure impressions in Search Console |
| TikTok / YouTube Shorts / Reels | "Here's why your bill went up by £11" with a real (own) bill decomposed on screen is shareable | 10 shorts over two cap windows; measure saves/shares and landing CTR |
| YouTube long-form | Explainers around cap dates | 2 videos; partner with a UK personal-finance creator |
| Reddit (r/UKPersonalFinance, r/OctopusEnergy, r/unitedkingdom) & MSE forum | Genuine answers to "why is my bill higher?" threads build credibility | Answer by hand, disclose affiliation, link only where rules allow |
| Personal-finance creators | Audiences overlap exactly | Offer a free explainer segment, no paid promo until retention proven |
| Consumer advocacy (Citizens Advice local offices, fuel-poverty charities) | Advisers need a quick way to show clients why bills changed | 3 adviser interviews → pilot |
| Energy-saving communities | Low — they already use smart-meter tools | Deprioritise |

## 10 short-form ideas

1. "Your October bill went down… but prices went up. Here's the maths." (VAT cut vs cap rise)
2. "One extra day on your bill costs you this much" (period length effect)
3. "The part of your electricity bill you can't reduce by using less" (standing charge share)
4. "Is your bill an estimate? Look for this letter." (E vs A readings)
5. "The 12-month back-billing rule in 30 seconds"
6. "Economy 7: are you actually saving? The break-even in one number"
7. "Is your Direct Debit too high? A 3-step check"
8. "Why your bill doesn't match your smart meter display" (cut-off dates, standing charge)
9. "April 2027: why your electricity bill may jump again" (VAT scheduled to return)
10. "I split my bill increase into 5 reasons — which one is yours?"

## Creator hypothesis

A UK personal-finance creator with 50k–500k followers demonstrating their own bill decomposition around a cap date yields ≥ 1,000 installs with ≥ 30% activation. Test once, unpaid or at fixed fee, no affiliate.

## Referral hypothesis

Users share a result card ("My bill went up £11.25: £8.10 usage, £5.19 prices, −£5.34 VAT") with household members/friends. V1 has no share feature (no network needed, but deliberately deferred to avoid sharing sensitive data by accident). Test: add an optional image share with amounts only; measure shares per activated user (≥ 0.1 ⇒ worthwhile).
