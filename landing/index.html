Why did my electricity bill change?

Add two bills. EnergyLens splits the difference into plain reasons — each with its pound amount — and they add up exactly.

Worked example illustrative figures, not a real customer

September bill £95.50 → October bill £106.75: £11.25 more

Used more per day (10.0 to 11.0 kWh)+£8.10
Higher price per kWh (24.87p to 26.32p before VAT)+£5.19
Billing period 1 day longer+£3.18
Higher daily standing charge+£0.12
VAT rate changed (5% to 0%)−£5.34

Calculated from the figures on the two bills. Weather and season change usage, so a usage increase isn't proof anything went wrong.

What it does

  • Recalculates your bill and checks it against the total printed on it.
  • Handles bills that span a price change and Economy 7 day/night rates.
  • Paste text from a PDF bill — it finds the figures, you check them.
  • "What if?": what a tariff quote would have cost for your real usage.
  • "Is my Direct Debit right?" — clearly labelled as an estimate.
  • Pick one thing to try, and your next bill shows whether it changed.

Private and independent

  • Works on your phone only. No account, no supplier login, no internet access.
  • Never asks for your name, address, account number or MPAN.
  • Not your supplier, not Ofgem, not a comparison site. No commission, no ads.

What it won't do

It won't guarantee savings, pretend to know what each appliance costs, or tell you which supplier to pick. Some features are estimates, and the app says so.
