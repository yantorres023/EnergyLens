**App name (≤30):** EnergyLens: Why my bill changed
**Subtitle / short description (≤80):** See why your electricity bill changed, in pounds. Private, on your phone.

**Full description:**

Your electricity bill went up (or down) — but why?

Add two bills and EnergyLens splits the difference into plain reasons, each with its pound amount:
• more or fewer days in the billing period
• using more or less electricity per day
• a higher or lower price per kWh
• a change in the daily standing charge
• VAT changes (such as the cut to 0% on domestic electricity from 1 October 2026)
• credits and one-off charges

The parts always add up to the exact difference.

Also:
• Check our maths against the total printed on your bill
• Bills that span a price change (two sets of prices) and Economy 7 day/night rates
• Paste text from a PDF bill — we find the figures, you check them
• "What if?": see what a tariff quote would have cost for your real usage
• "Is my Direct Debit right?" — an estimate from your own bills
• Pick one thing to try; your next bill shows whether it changed

Private by design: no account, no login to your supplier, no name/address/account number, no internet access, no ads, no tracking.

Honest by design: every number shows where it came from (you entered it, calculated, or an estimate). EnergyLens is independent of energy suppliers and Ofgem, does not sell tariffs or earn commission, and cannot guarantee savings.

For households in England, Scotland and Wales with a credit (non-prepayment) electricity meter.
